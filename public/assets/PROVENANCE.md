# Asset provenance

On 2026-11-12 the in-game Gemini illustrations listed next were moved to `art-archive/gemini/` (kept, not bundled) and replaced by project-authored vectors so the game has one style; the app icon set is unchanged. Former in-game raster assets under `public/assets/` (`bg-garden-day.jpg`, `bg-garden-night.jpg`, `bg-pond.jpg`, `bg-valley-map.jpg`, `puppy.png`, `reindeer.png`, `house.png`, `tree.png`, `cloud.png`, `sun.png`) and the app icon set (`public/favicon.ico`, `public/icon-32.png`, `public/icon-180.png`, `public/icon-192.png`, `public/icon-512.png`) were generated on 2026-11-11 with Gemini (`gemini-3.1-flash-image`) in a cozy storybook gouache and soft moss-ink (`#52684e`) style. The app icon features Lucky the chubby white cartoon puppy hugging a striped watermelon on a full-bleed sunlit wildflower meadow with no text or borders, resized with `sips` and packed to ICO. Isolated object PNGs were background-removed via edge flood-fill and exported at 512×512 RGBA with 7% padding and bottom-center anchors. Remaining objects in `src/components/Art.tsx` use upgraded project-authored gradient SVG artwork pending the remaining Gemini generation batch. No third-party copyrighted character was intentionally incorporated. The repository owner must verify applicable terms and choose a distribution license before broad release.

`public/favicon.svg`, `public/icon-maskable-192.png` and `public/icon-maskable-512.png` are derived from `public/icon-512.png` by `scripts/make-icons.mjs` (2026-11-11): the SVG wraps a 128px copy with rounded corners; the maskable icons place the illustration at 78% over a blurred copy of itself. No new generated content was added.

The interface font is Nunito, bundled from the `@fontsource/nunito` package (SIL Open Font License 1.1) with the `latin` and `vietnamese` subsets at weights 400, 600, 700 and 800. Interface icons in `src/components/Icons.tsx` were authored for this project; the Google mark on the sign-in button is Google's logo and is used only to identify that provider.
